Cooperative-store alternative: cross-GPU CI screening
=====================================================

This branch contains the standalone cooperative-store experiment plus the CPU
replay alias fix, based on a8572d7. It does NOT include stream-density changes.
It is the ungated experiment used for comparison, not a cross-GPU production fix.

Source build 2609.1903 (158104899) triggered fleet 20260919.3 (158105512).

RX 9070 has no valid ladder: its legacy test timed out before performance
measurement in the candidate and controls. Other recorded provisioning/harness
issues also occur in controls. No new CI gtest failure was identified; skipped
tests remain and are not counted as successful coverage.

Conclusion: cooperative stores also need vendor gating. They recover RX 6900 XT
throughput but give up existing gains on every other measured GPU in this screen.
The preferred fix remains AMD-gated stream density, not this global alternative.
