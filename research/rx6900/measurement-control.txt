Measurement control
===================

Remote.ps1 checks <remote campaign root>\pause-before-perf.json before starting
a Perf or Profile operation. If present, it throws the JSON object's reason.
An invocation already running is unaffected. This lets the coordinator stop an
obsolete multi-ladder schedule at its next complete-ladder boundary without
killing a GPU process or invalidating completed observations.

The evaluator remains the sole measurement owner. After reading and adopting the
new coordinator policy, it may remove that exact pause file and resume. Do not
interpret the intentional pause, or a missing result for a never-started RunId,
as a product failure. Retain completed schedule rows and original raw results.

Policy version 2 (user requested faster evaluation):
- Screen candidates using one complete exact-CI ladder, with fresh baseline
  observations bracketing the screening block.
- Keep the original corpus, eight rungs, five sweeps and pooling unchanged.
- Reuse completed observations; do not repeat obvious losing candidates.
- Screening is provisional ranking, not statistical acceptance.
- Reserve full repeated confirmation and final correctness gates for promising
  finalists against the incumbent and fresh PR156 reference.

The latest coordinator policy is in the local campaign knowledge\campaign.json.
